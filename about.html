About

I'm Joshua Kim. I studied computer science with a minor in design, which is a long way of saying I never wanted to pick a side.

Most of what I make lives on the web. I'm drawn to projects where the interface is the hard part — where getting the structure right matters as much as getting the code right. I care about the small stuff: how a page loads, where the eye lands first, whether the thing explains itself without a manual.

Outside of that, I'm usually taking apart something I didn't need to take apart. This site is a case in point — hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no framework, because building it myself was the whole point.
