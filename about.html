About

I'm Joshua Kim. I studied computer science at Washington University in St. Louis with a minor in design, and most of what I've done since has been an attempt to keep both halves in the same job.

In practice that means I tend to build the whole thing. SpikeReport is a publication where I designed the site, wrote the publishing system, and write the articles. FSLR is a parody ranking body that only works because the design plays it completely straight. Before those, I wrote gameplay code for a Unity endless runner with three other people. The through-line isn't a stack — it's wanting the thing to feel considered all the way down.

I also spend a lot of time explaining this stuff to people who don't have my background. I taught an AI literacy course to thirty high schoolers with no coding experience, getting them from "what's a language model" to publishing a working mobile app. Before that I was a TA for web development and intro computer engineering. Teaching has a way of showing you which of your explanations were never explanations at all.

This site is hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No framework, no build step — building it from scratch was the whole point.
